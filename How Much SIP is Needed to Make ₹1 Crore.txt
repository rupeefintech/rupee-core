# What is SIP?

SIP (Systematic Investment Plan) allows you to invest a fixed amount regularly into Mutual Funds.

Instead of investing a lump sum, you invest:
- Monthly
- Quarterly
- Regularly over time

SIPs are popular because they make wealth creation simple and disciplined.

# Understanding Compounding

Compounding means:

Your money earns returns,
and those returns also earn additional returns.

Over long periods, wealth growth accelerates rapidly.

---

```html
<div class="highlight-box">

Example:

₹10,000 monthly SIP at 12% annual return:

• 10 Years → ~₹23 lakh  
• 15 Years → ~₹50 lakh  
• 20 Years → ~₹1 crore  

Most wealth creation happens during later years because compounding grows exponentially.

</div>
```

# What is Step-Up SIP?

A Step-Up SIP means increasing your SIP amount every year.

Example:
- Start SIP with ₹5,000/month
- Increase by 10% annually

This strategy aligns investing with salary growth.

---

```html
<div class="highlight-box">

A ₹10,000 SIP with annual 10% increase may generate significantly more wealth than a fixed SIP over long durations.

</div>
```

# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

## Can ₹5,000 SIP make ₹1 crore?

Yes, potentially over very long durations like 25–30 years depending on returns.

---

## Is SIP guaranteed to give ₹1 crore?

No. Mutual Fund returns are market-linked and not guaranteed.

---

## Which SIP is best for ₹1 crore goal?

Long-term diversified equity mutual funds are commonly preferred for wealth creation goals.

---

## What if market crashes during SIP?

Market volatility is normal. Long-term SIP investing often benefits from market corrections.

---

## Can I increase SIP later?

Yes. Step-Up SIP allows increasing investment amounts annually.
