# What Is SIP?

SIP means investing a fixed amount regularly into mutual funds.

Instead of investing ₹1 lakh at one time, SIP allows you to invest monthly.

Examples:

* ₹1,000 every month
* ₹5,000 every month
* ₹10,000 every month

This builds investing discipline and reduces market timing risk.

# Best SIP Strategy for Salaried Employees

* Start SIP immediately after salary credit
* Increase SIP yearly with salary hikes
* Invest for long-term goals
* Avoid stopping during market volatility

> 💡 **Pro Tip:** Increasing SIP by just 10% yearly can dramatically increase long-term wealth.
