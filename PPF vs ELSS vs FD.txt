# What is PPF?

PPF (Public Provident Fund) is a government-backed long-term savings scheme.

It is popular because of:

✅ Guaranteed returns  
✅ Tax-free maturity  
✅ Very low risk  
✅ Long-term stability

PPF is often used for:
- Retirement planning
- Safe wealth accumulation
- Long-term savings

---

# What is ELSS?

ELSS (Equity Linked Savings Scheme) is a type of Mutual Fund that primarily invests in stock markets.

It offers:

✅ Tax-saving benefits under Section 80C  
✅ Market-linked returns  
✅ Higher long-term growth potential

ELSS is suitable for:
- Wealth creation
- Long-term investing
- Inflation-beating returns

---

# What is Fixed Deposit (FD)?

Fixed Deposit is a traditional bank investment offering fixed interest over a specified period.

FDs are known for:

✅ Safety  
✅ Guaranteed returns  
✅ Simplicity

They are commonly preferred by:
- Conservative investors
- Senior citizens
- Short-term savers

# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

## Is ELSS better than PPF?

ELSS may offer higher long-term returns, while PPF offers higher safety and tax-free maturity.

---

## Is PPF completely tax-free?

Yes. PPF enjoys EEE status under current tax rules.

---

## Which is safer — ELSS or FD?

FD is safer because returns are fixed, while ELSS is market-linked.

---

## Can I invest in all three?

Yes. Many investors diversify using PPF, ELSS, and FD together.

---

## Which is best for retirement?

A combination of PPF and long-term ELSS investing can help balance safety and growth.
